Revisa offline Portuguese (Brazil) suggestion dictionary

Derived from VERO, the Brazilian Portuguese Hunspell dictionary distributed
by LibreOffice. Copyright (C) 2006-2013 Raimundo Santos Moura and contributors.
The upstream notices and credits are retained in README_pt_BR.txt and
README_en.txt. This dictionary data is redistributed under LGPL version 3;
the license texts are COPYING.LESSER and COPYING.

Pinned source:
https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/1e848fbddd7fd8e03fb696ecc03ee1068fab141c/pt_BR

Revisa modifications: export of standalone lowercase words and affix-generated
forms, filtering of unsupported compound tokens and suggestion exclusions,
accent-insensitive ordering, verb-form links, and conversion to a front-coded
binary index compressed with gzip. No user text or learned vocabulary is stored.

The original pt_BR.dic and pt_BR.aff and the complete conversion script are
provided in tools/dictionary/source and tools/dictionary/build_dictionary.py
in the Revisa project distributed alongside this generated dictionary.
The asset is reproducible with Python 3 (standard library only); no network is
needed to regenerate it. manifest.json records source and asset checksums.
